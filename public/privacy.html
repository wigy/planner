Privacy policy

planner is a private, self-hosted todo board, calendar and time bookkeeping tool. Only the people its operator has listed can sign in. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Signing in with Google gives planner your name, your email address and access to your Google Calendar. It reads your calendars to show them, and it creates, changes and deletes events only when you do so in planner. It keeps a copy of your calendar events in its own database so that it can show and report on them.

What it stores

Your todo cards and comments, your projects and a mirror of your calendar events are stored on the operator's own server and in its encrypted backups. The Google refresh token is stored encrypted. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No tracking cookies are used; one cookie keeps you signed in.

Removing access

You can revoke planner's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and ask the operator to delete your data.

planner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
